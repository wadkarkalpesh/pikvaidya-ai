## 1. Crop Overview & Soil Management
Sugarcane in Maharashtra is grown predominantly in medium to heavy black cotton soils (Vertisols) with good drainage and pH 6.8 to 8.0. For optimum ratoon and adsali crops (Co 86032, Phule 0265, CoM 0265), deep summer ploughing followed by rotavator pulverization and application of 25 tonnes/ha of well-decomposed Farmyard Manure (FYM) enriched with Trichoderma harzianum (2.5 kg/ha) is recommended. Ridge and furrow spacing of 120 cm to 150 cm facilitates mechanization, trash mulching, and efficient sub-surface drip irrigation.

## 2. Sett Treatment Protocol (बीजप्रक्रिया)
Primary source of red rot, smut, and grassy shoot transmission is infected seed setts.
- **Moist Hot Air Treatment:** Treat seed setts at 54°C for 2.5 hours with 95% relative humidity to eradicate intracellular phytoplasmas (Grassy shoot) and latent smut mycelium.
- **Chemical Sett Dip:** Dip two-eye bud setts for 15 minutes before planting in a solution of Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1.0 g per litre of water OR Benomyl 50% WP @ 1.0 g per litre of water.
- **Biological Sett Inoculation:** Followed by dipping for 30 minutes in Acetobacter diazotrophicus (liquid biofertilizer @ 2.5 litres/ha) and Phosphobacteria (2.5 litres/ha) mixed in 100 litres of water.

## 3. Red Rot Management (तांबडे कूज रोग नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Colletotrichum falcatum Went (Glomerella tucumanensis).
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing and drying of the 3rd or 4th leaf from top; spindle leaves wither prematurely. Longitudinal splitting of the stalk exhibits dark red pith discoloration with diagnostic transverse white chalky bands and alcoholic odour.
- **Epidemiology:** Spread through irrigation water, contaminated setts, and rain splash during monsoons (July to October) with temperature 28–32°C and RH > 85%.
- **Cultural & Biological Control:**
  1. Grow resistant cultivars such as Co 86032 (Nira) and CoM 0265 (Phule 265). Strictly avoid susceptible varieties in endemic zones.
  2. Implement 2-year crop rotation with green manure crops (dhaincha or sunnhemp) or paddy.
  3. Eradicate and burn affected clumps immediately along with roots.
  4. Soil application of Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 5 kg/ha mixed in 500 kg FYM during earthing up.
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved):**
  - Sett treatment: Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1.0 g/litre for 15 minutes.
  - Foliar/Soil drench at early onset: Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP @ 1.0 g/litre or Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1.0 g/litre.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 30 days.

## 4. Sugarcane Rust Management (तांबेरा रोग नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Puccinia melanocephala Syd. & P. Syd.
- **Symptoms:** Initial minute, elongated chlorotic yellowish flecks on both leaf surfaces, turning into elongated reddish-brown to orange-brown pustules. Urediniospores erupt through epidermis, producing powdery rusty appearance, causing necrosis and drying.
- **Favourable Conditions:** Cool nights (15–20°C) with high relative humidity (>80%) and dew formation during winter/autumn (November to February).
- **Management Measures:**
  - Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization (keep within MPKV recommended 250 kg N/ha for suru, 400 kg N/ha for adsali).
  - Foliar spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre of water OR Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/litre of water.
  - Repeat after 15 days if cloudy weather and dew persist.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21–30 days.

---

## 5. Drip Irrigation Scheduling & Trash Mulching
- Apply 8 to 10 tonnes/ha of sugarcane trash in alternate furrows after first earthing up. Trash mulching conserves 25–30% soil moisture, suppresses weed germination, and buffers soil temperature.
- Water requirement: 1,800 to 2,200 mm per annual cycle. Under drip irrigation, schedule water at 0.8 Kc during formative stage, 1.1 Kc during grand growth phase, and 0.6 Kc during maturity. Avoid water stagnation to prevent fungal root rots.
