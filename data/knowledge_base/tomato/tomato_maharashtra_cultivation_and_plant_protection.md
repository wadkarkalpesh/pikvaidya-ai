## 1. Nursery Management & Raised Bed Planting
In Maharashtra's heavy Vertisols, direct seeding or flatbed transplanting leads to high seedling mortality due to damping-off (Pythium/Rhizoctonia).
- Prepare raised beds of 1 metre width, 15 cm height, and convenient length.
- Solarize nursery soil with 25-micron transparent polyethylene film for 30 days during May.
- Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/kg seed or Thiram 75% WP @ 3 g/kg before sowing.
- Transplant 25 to 30 days old vigorous seedlings on raised beds with drip irrigation and silver-black polyethylene mulch (30-micron) at 120 cm x 60 cm spacing. Staking with bamboo and trellising wires prevents ground fruit contact and minimizes foliar diseases.

## 2. Early Blight Management (अल्टरनेरिया करपा रोग नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Alternaria solani Sorauer.
- **Symptoms:** Characteristic circular to oval brown necrotic lesions on older leaves displaying prominent concentric target-like rings ("target-board" effect). Surrounding leaf tissue shows chlorotic yellow halo. Causes premature defoliation and sunken dark leathery rot at the stem end of fruits.
- **Favourable Conditions:** Alternating wet and dry periods, warm temperatures (24–30°C), and relative humidity > 80%.
- **Cultural & Biological Control:**
  1. Follow 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (maize, sorghum, cowpea).
  2. Stake plants to elevate foliage above soil splash line.
  3. Prune bottom 30 cm senescent leaves to enhance airflow and lower canopy humidity.
  4. Foliar spray of Trichoderma harzianum or Bacillus subtilis @ 5 g/litre of water at initial symptom manifestation.
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved):**
  - Preventative: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/litre.
  - Curative / Systemic: Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/litre OR Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/litre.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 3 to 5 days for Azoxystrobin/Difenoconazole; 7 days for Mancozeb.

## 3. Late Blight Management (उशिरा येणारा करपा / लेट ब्लाइट नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Phytophthora infestans (Mont.) de Bary.
- **Symptoms:** Water-soaked irregular greenish-black lesions on leaf margins and tips that rapidly enlarge under cool, moist conditions. White downy/cottony fungal mildew develops on leaf undersides under high humidity. Stems turn dark brown to black; fruits develop large, firm, greasy olive-brown blotches.
- **Favourable Conditions:** Prolonged cloudy skies, temperatures 12–22°C, relative humidity > 90%, and free leaf surface moisture (heavy dew or drizzling rain).
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved):**
  - Prophylactic: Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre.
  - Systemic at Outbreak: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/litre OR Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/litre OR Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/litre.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 7 to 10 days.

## 4. Bacterial Spot Management (जीवाणूजन्य करपा / ठिपके)
- **Causal Organism:** Xanthomonas vesicatoria (Doidge).
- **Symptoms:** Small, water-soaked, dark brown to black circular lesions on leaves, often surrounded by a prominent yellow halo. Fruit lesions appear as small, raised, blister-like black spots that become scab-like with rough brown centers.
- **Control Measures:**
  - Seed treatment with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g (100 ppm) per litre of water for 30 minutes.
  - Foliar spray of Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/litre combined with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/litre.
  - Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation; always utilize drip systems.

## 5. Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus & Whitefly IPM (चुरडा-मुरडा / पांढरी माशी)
- **Vector:** Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci Gennadius).
- **Symptoms:** Severe stunting of plants, upward curling, cupping, and crinkling of leaf margins, yellowing of lamina, and complete reduction of fruit set.
- **IPM Measures:**
  1. Grow border crop of 2 rows of maize or bajra 20 days prior to transplanting to act as barrier.
  2. Install yellow sticky traps @ 20 to 25 traps per hectare at canopy height.
  3. Foliar spray of Neem oil (10,000 ppm) @ 2.0 ml/litre or Pongamia oil @ 3.0 ml/litre at early vegetative stage.
  4. Vector chemical management: Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/litre OR Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/litre. Rotate with Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD @ 1.8 ml/litre.
  5. Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 3 to 5 days.
