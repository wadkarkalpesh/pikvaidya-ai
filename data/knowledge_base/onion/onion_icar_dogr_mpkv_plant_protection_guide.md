## 1. Agro-Techniques & Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) Cultivation
In Maharashtra, kharif, late kharif (rangda), and rabi onion crops suffer heavy root and foliar disease losses under flatbed flooding.
- **Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) Planting:** Prepare beds of 120 cm top width, 15 cm furrow depth, spaced 30 cm apart. BBF prevents waterlogging, enhances root aeration, and lowers collar rot / damping-off incidence by 40%.
- **Seedling Age:** Transplant 45 to 50 days old healthy seedlings. Pruning seedling tops prior to transplanting is discouraged by ICAR-DOGR as open wounds invite fungal infection.
- **Sulphur & Potash Nutrition:** Apply 20 to 25 kg/ha elemental sulphur at land preparation. Sulphur enhances pyruvic acid and allicin synthesis, imparting biochemical resistance against fungal attack. Apply Potassium (50 kg K2O/ha) to thicken bulb scales and prevent storage rots.

## 2. Purple Blotch Management (जांभळा करपा रोग नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Alternaria porri (Ellis) Cif.
- **Symptoms:** Initial small, sunken, water-soaked whitish flecks on tubular leaves. Lesions rapidly enlarge into elliptical to spindle-shaped purple to dark brown patches with deep reddish-purple margins, surrounded by a broad chlorotic yellow zone. Leaves turn yellow, collapse, and dry from tips downwards.
- **Epidemiology:** High relative humidity (80–90%), temperatures between 21–30°C, and prolonged leaf wetness (rain or overhead sprinklers).
- **Non-Chemical Management:**
  1. Use certified seeds of tolerant varieties like Bhima Super, Bhima Dark Red, Phule Samarth, or Baswant 780.
  2. Implement drip irrigation; avoid late-afternoon overhead micro-sprinkler irrigation that keeps leaves wet through the night.
  3. Install sticky traps and avoid excessive nitrogen application (>100 kg N/ha).
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved):**
  - Note: Onion foliage has a heavy waxy cuticle; ALWAYS add non-ionic wetting agent / sticker (e.g. Sandovit / Apsa-80 @ 0.5 ml/litre).
  - Prophylactic: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre OR Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/litre.
  - Curative: Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml/litre OR Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.75 ml/litre OR Azoxystrobin 11% + Tebuconazole 18.3% SC @ 1.0 ml/litre.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 10 to 14 days.

## 3. Stemphylium Leaf Blight (स्टेमफिलियम करपा नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Organism:** Stemphylium vesicarium (Wallr.) E.G. Simmons.
- **Symptoms:** Small, light yellow to ochre water-soaked spots on the outer side of leaves that elongate into elongated spindle-shaped straw-coloured necrotic lesions. As spores mature, centers turn dark olive-brown to black with dense soot-like fungal growth. Lesions coalesce causing severe blighting of foliage.
- **Distinction from Purple Blotch:** Stemphylium lesions lack the deep concentric purple coloration and exhibit straw-yellow centers with dense black powdery sporulation.
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved):**
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/litre OR Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/litre with non-ionic sticker @ 0.5 ml/litre.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 15 days.

## 4. Iris Yellow Spot Virus (IYSV) & Thrips Management (फुलकिडे व आयवायएसव्ही नियंत्रण)
- **Causal Agent:** Iris Yellow Spot Tospovirus transmitted solely by Onion Thrips (Thrips tabaci Lindeman).
- **Symptoms:** Diagnostic chlorotic diamond-shaped or straw-coloured eye-spot lesions with green islands in the center on flower stalks (scapes) and leaves. Scapes bend, lodge, and break easily, causing catastrophic seed crop failure and stunted bulbs.
- **Vector Control & IPM:**
  1. Plant two border rows of maize or maize + pearl millet 15 days before transplanting as barrier.
  2. Install blue sticky traps @ 25 to 30 traps/hectare (blue traps attract 2.5x more Thrips tabaci than yellow traps).
  3. Chemical control of thrips: Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 ml/litre OR Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.8 ml/litre OR Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD @ 1.2 ml/litre. Rotate insecticide classes to prevent insecticide resistance.
  4. Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 7 to 10 days.
