# PikVaidya AI — Progressive Web App (PWA) User Guide
*(शेतकरी व कृषी सहाय्यकांसाठी वापर मार्गदर्शक)*

## 1. Introduction (परिचय)

**PikVaidya AI** is now installable as a native app on mobile phones, tablets, and computers, functioning without requiring constant cellular internet.

पिकवैद्य AI आता मोबाईल, टॅबलेट किंवा संगणकावर एका ॲपप्रमाणे इन्स्टॉल करता येते आणि शेतात इंटरनेट नसतानाही काम करते.

---

## 2. Installing the App (ॲप कसे इन्स्टॉल करावे)

### Android (Google Chrome):
1. Open Chrome and visit the PikVaidya AI portal (`http://<your-server>:3000` or public URL).
2. Tap the **"📲 Install PikVaidya App"** button at the top of the screen (or tap the 3 dots in Chrome and select **"Add to Home screen"** / **"Install app"**).
3. The app icon 🌿 will appear on your mobile home screen.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari):
1. Open the website in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.

---

## 3. Using Offline Edge AI (इंटरनेटशिवाय रोग निदान कसे करावे)

1. **Pre-load Models while connected**:
   - While you have WiFi or mobile data in the village or home, select your crop (**Sugarcane / Tomato / Onion**).
   - If you see `📦 Download Model for Offline`, tap it once. The 11.8MB AI model will be safely stored in your phone's memory.
2. **Go to the Field**:
   - You can now take your phone into remote sugarcane or onion fields with zero connectivity.
   - Take a clear photo of the infected leaf.
   - Tap **"⚡ Diagnose On-Device (Zero Internet)"** (`इंटरनेटशिवाय फोनवर निदान करा`).
   - In less than 1 second, your phone will identify the crop disease, show confidence percentage, and display immediate emergency cultural recommendations from ICAR!
3. **Automatic Cloud Sync**:
   - When you return to an area with mobile network or WiFi, PikVaidya AI will automatically upload your saved scans in the background.
   - Full Grad-CAM visual heatmaps and official CIBRC pesticide spray advisory PDFs will sync immediately into your **Diagnosis History** (`तपासणी इतिहास`)!

---

## 4. Viewing & Exporting Diagnosis History (इतिहास कसा पहावा व एक्सपोर्ट करावा)

1. Tap **"📋 Offline Diagnostic History"** (`तपासणी इतिहास`) in the top navigation bar.
2. View all past scans, dates, and diagnosed conditions.
3. Tap **"📥 Export History (JSON)"** to save a complete backup file to your phone's storage.
