# PikVaidya AI — Farmer & Agronomist Assistant User Guide
===========================================================

**Guide Version:** 1.0  
**Languages:** English | मराठी (Marathi) | हिंदी (Hindi)  

---

## 1. Introduction
The PikVaidya AI Farmer Assistant is an intelligent agronomic advisor designed specifically for sugarcane, tomato, and onion growers across Maharashtra. It bridges cutting-edge computer vision disease diagnosis with verified university packages of practices (MPKV Rahuri, ICAR-DOGR, ICAR-SBI, Dr. PDKV Akola) and real-time weather safety checks.

---

## 2. Key Features

1. **Multilingual Interaction:**
   - Speak or type naturally in **Marathi (मराठी)**, **Hindi (हिंदी)**, or **English**.
   - The assistant auto-detects your language and replies in the same tongue.

2. **Diagnosis Context Integration:**
   - When you upload a leaf photo, your scan results (crop type, identified disease, confidence, and severity) are automatically sent to the assistant.
   - You can simply ask follow-up questions like:
     - *"औषध काय फवारावे?"* (What medicine should I spray?)
     - *"डोस किती द्यावा?"* (What is the dosage?)
     - *"What is the organic treatment?"*

3. **Weather-Aware Spray Safety:**
   - If local weather conditions show imminent rain or high wind (> 15 km/h), the assistant actively warns you to **hold spraying** to avoid chemical runoff and waste.

4. **100% Verified CIBRC Dosages:**
   - Zero invented chemicals. Every chemical fungicide or insecticide specifies the official dosage per litre and the Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) in days.

5. **Mandatory Protective Equipment (PPE):**
   - Reminds growers to wear nitrile gloves, masks, and eye protection under the Insecticides Rules, 1971.

## 3. Example Dialogues

### Example 1: Marathi (मराठी) — Sugarcane Red Rot
- **शेतकरी:** `"उसावरील तांबडे कूज रोगावर कोणती बेणेप्रक्रिया करावी?"`
- **पिकवैद्य सहाय्यक:**
  > अधिकृत ICAR/CIBRC माहितीनुसार, **Red Rot** (Colletotrichum falcatum) बाबत खालील उपाययोजना प्रमाणित आहेत:  
  > 
  > लागवडीपूर्वी बेणे कार्बेन्डाझिम ५०% WP @ १ ग्रॅम प्रति लिटर पाण्यात १५ मिनिटे बुडवून बेणेप्रक्रिया करावी. शेतात रोगाची सुरुवातीची लक्षणे दिसल्यास थायोफिनेट मिथाईल ७०% WP @ १ ग्रॅम प्रति लिटर आळवणी करावी. काढणीपूर्वीचा कालावधी (PHI): ३० दिवस.  
  > 
  > **संदर्भ:** ICAR-SBI Coimbatore & MPKV Rahuri  
  > **वैधानिक सूचना:** कीटकनाशक कायदा १९६८ नुसार वेष्टनावरील सूचनांचे पालन करावे.

### Example 2: English — Onion Purple Blotch with Adverse Weather
- **Farmer:** `"What should I spray for purple blotch in onion?"`
- **PikVaidya Assistant:**
  > According to official ICAR-DOGR and CIBRC packages of practices for **Purple Blotch** (*Alternaria porri*): [⚠️ Weather Advisory: Current microclimate is unfavourable for spraying due to rain/wind hazard.]  
  > 
  > - Add non-ionic sticker (0.5 ml/L) due to waxy leaf bloom.  
  > - Prophylactic: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L.  
  > - Curative: Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.75 ml/L.  
  > - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 10 to 14 days.  
  > 
  > **Sources:** ICAR-DOGR Rajgurunagar & MPKV Rahuri.

## 4. Safety Guardrails & Expert Referral
- **Low Confidence Notice:** If the image diagnosis has moderate/low confidence (< 70%), the assistant alerts you:  
  *"Please consult your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Taluka Agriculture Officer before applying chemical treatments."*
- **Out-of-Scope Questions:** The assistant will not answer questions unrelated to agriculture, crops, or farming.
